Get Pip

Who is Pip?

Pip is a little pixel-inspired pigeon who wanders around your Windows desktop, says silly things and keeps you company.

He can even chat with you using a locally running AI model. Give him some bread and make his day! 🍞

What can Pip do?

🚶

Wanders Around

Pip strolls around your desktop while you work.

💬

Little Messages

Click Pip to hear his silly pigeon thoughts.

🤖

AI Chat

Right-click Pip to open his AI chat window.

🍞

Bread Time!

Give Pip bread and watch him get excited!

What you'll need

  1. Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Python 3.10 or newer (for the Python Edition).
  3. Ollama installed from ollama.com.
  4. The AI model downloaded using: ollama pull llama3.2:3b
  5. An internet connection for the initial downloads.
